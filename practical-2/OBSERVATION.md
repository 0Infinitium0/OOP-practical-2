# Result / Observation

Successfully designed and implemented a Bank Account class using private and public access specifiers.

A constructor was used to initialize account details. Public member functions were created to display account information, deposit money and withdraw money. Two objects, Satinderpal and Priya, were created and their member functions were demonstrated successfully.

The private members protect account information from direct access, while public member functions provide controlled access to the data. The program also validates deposit and withdrawal amounts and prevents withdrawals greater than the available balance.

This practical helped in understanding classes, objects, constructors, member functions and encapsulation in C++.
